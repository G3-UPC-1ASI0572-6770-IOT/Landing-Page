Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how ParkingNow collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when users access our landing site, request information, or use connected parking services.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect identification and contact data (name, email), user profile data (driver or owner), usage and analytics data, technical logs (browser, device, timestamps), and communication content submitted through forms or support channels.

3. How We Use Data

Data is used to provide platform features, process inquiries, improve reliability, detect fraud or misuse, maintain security, and communicate operational updates or legally required notices.

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on user consent, performance of requested services, legitimate interest in service improvement and security, and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with infrastructure providers, analytics tools, payment facilitators, and legal authorities when required, under strict confidentiality and contractual safeguards.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as needed to fulfill service purposes, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods are periodically reviewed.

7. Security Measures

ParkingNow applies technical and organizational controls including access restrictions, encrypted transport channels, monitoring, and incident response procedures to reduce unauthorized access risk.

8. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability where applicable. You may also object to certain processing activities and withdraw consent at any time.

9. International Transfers

If data is processed outside your jurisdiction, we apply appropriate contractual and technical protections to maintain equivalent privacy safeguards.
